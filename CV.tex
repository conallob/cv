\subsubsection*{Senior II Site Reliability Engineer (September 2024 - Current)}

Crusoe.ai is a sustainable energy focused "neocloud" Cloud computing provider, operating in the AI infrastructure space. They provide bare-metal,
VM and managed GPU-as-a-service solutions for LLM training and LLM Inference, powered by NVIDIA (H100, H200, B200, GB200, etc)
and AMD (MI355X, etc) GPUs.

I was the first engineer and third employee hired outside of the US. Worked with local manager \& recruiter to help grow the local SRE team to 15
\& the local office to 35+.

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
	\item Overhauled SRE onboarding, replacing tribal knowledge with structured documentation, recorded talks, and a peer forum of new
		hires helping new hires --- measurably reducing ramp time for every subsequent hire.
	\item Designed \& implemented a multi-environment (VM, bare-metal, Kubernetes) synthetic network observability tool built on
		Cloudprober with custom extensions; adopted by 8+ internal teams. Contributed upstream fixes to open-source Cloudprober.
	\item Recognised PromQL and observability expert across SRE, multiple engineering teams, and Customer Success.
	\item Instrumented Ansible playbooks to expose firmware manifest state via \texttt{node\_exporter}, enabling PromQL-gated alerts ---
		reducing alert volume from 100+ pages per 12-hour shift to $\sim$1--2 during active firmware rollouts.
	\item Extended Ansible playbook instrumentation to publish precise per-node start and completion timestamps, providing automatic
		change-correlation breadcrumbs that dramatically reduced mean time to identify the causative change during multi-node incidents.
	\item Redesigned OVSDB Relay architecture from a brittle per-node \texttt{readinessProbe} setup to a NEG L4 load balancer with
		weighted TCP health checks and Kubernetes horizontal autoscaling --- eliminating repeated paging on OVN Controller turn-up
		and achieving 10$\times$ SDN scalability.
	\item Reached oncall readiness across 4 distinct rotations within 12 months, commanding 20+ incidents across Cloud Storage,
		Incident Commander, and SDN --- reflecting consistent ability to absorb complex, unfamiliar systems rapidly.
\end{itemize}
